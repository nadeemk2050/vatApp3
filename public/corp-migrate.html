What this does

This tool copies the corporate financial data from the old CORP Firebase project (corporateapp-25ca4) into this VAT app project (vatapp-c80ce) under your currently signed-in VAT app account. It copies: companies, corporate history, finalized reports, and all saved drafts.

Existing documents with the same names in the destination will be overwritten. You must know the email and password of the account you used in the old CORP app.
